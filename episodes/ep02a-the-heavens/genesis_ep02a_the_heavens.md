## STORY PURPOSE
God divides the waters, and the first sky opens between them. The Deep of Episode 1 gains its first horizon, its first sense of distance, and a heaven above it. The episode ends with the world still entirely water, which sets up Day Three.

## THE CENTRAL CHALLENGE
"A firmament dividing the waters" invites clichés: a glass dome, a cosmic shockwave, clouds bursting apart in slow motion. Instead, the mist that lay on the water in Episode 1 **lifts as one physical body**. Water drains from its underside as it rises, and the air rushes into the gap. The drama comes from scale, weight and sound: the world acquires *space*.

### E02A-S02 · 0:03–0:09 · "LET THERE BE A FIRMAMENT"
- **Visual:** A low wide shot at water level; the mist lies on the sea like a ceiling that has come down to touch it. On the word *"firmament"* a **thin, dark seam of clear air** opens between water and mist across the *entire* width of the frame. **It is the first horizon line of the series.** The seam widens steadily as the whole mist-mass begins to lift as one body. Where it tears away from the water, long strands of water stretch, thin and snap, and fall back into the sea.
- **Purpose:** A locked-off camera lets the world obey on its own, and the stillness makes the change legible. The horizon appearing is a milestone the audience will feel without being told.
- **Camera:** 24mm, **locked-off**, about 1 m above the water. Water in the lower third; mist in the upper two-thirds.
- **Lighting:** Sourceless. The opening seam reads slightly darker at first: it is clear distance, where the mist had been luminous.
- **Audio:** A deep, slow *pull* of air: low wind drawn into the widening gap. Water strands falling back make a scattered splash texture. No music yet.
- **Voice:** GOD — KJV (0:02.6–0:08.7): *"Let there be a firmament in the midst of the waters,"*
- **Generation note:** use first-frame/last-frame control. First frame: mist touching water (S01's end). Last frame: a band of clear air a fifth of the frame high between sea and lifted mist.

## CONTINUITY NOTES
1. **E-02 is established.** From here on the world has a **horizon**, a clear expanse (sky) and very high "waters above". In later episodes the waters above read as a **high, thin luminous veil at the top of the sky**, never a low ceiling, so that on Day 4 the lights can be set "in the firmament" beneath it.
2. **Cross-arc callback:** the waters above return at the Flood (Genesis 7:11, "the windows of heaven were opened"). Keep this ceiling design on file for that episode.
3. **Still no sun, moon, stars or land.** Light remains sourceless and shadowless.
4. **No rain.** Water falling during the separation drains from the separating body only and stops once separation is complete (see 2:5–6).
5. **Day-ending device, Day 2:** water-level angle; darkness right to left, light from the left. One frame-drum stroke.
6. **M-01** now has a "widened" form. Use it for the heavens.
