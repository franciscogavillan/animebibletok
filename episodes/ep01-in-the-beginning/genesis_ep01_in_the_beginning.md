## STORY PURPOSE
Establish the series' voice: reverent, grounded, cinematic. Show a formless, dark world that God brings to order through speech alone, and the first light. The episode ends on the unfinished world, with the waters still undivided, which leads directly into Episode 2.

## THE CENTRAL CHALLENGE
With no characters and no sun, this is the episode most at risk of "AI spectacle" (glowing orbs, particle swarms, nebulae, god-rays). The answer is **physics and restraint.** The drama comes from real water, real wind, total silence, and one change: darkness becomes light. The world itself is the actor.

## SHOT-BY-SHOT

### E01-S01 · 0:00–0:06 · HOOK: "IN THE BEGINNING"
- **Visual:** The frame opens on featureless black, the upper void (the heaven). The camera tilts slowly down. On "the earth," the faintest blue-black sheen of a vast water surface enters the bottom of the frame: long, heavy swells, barely readable.
- **Environment:** E-01 before light. The only concession to visibility is a sourceless near-black blue, just enough to read shapes.
- **Camera:** 24mm equivalent. Slow, steady tilt down over the full 6 seconds.
- **Lighting:** None motivated; ambient near-black only.
- **Tone:** Vast, silent, primordial.
- **Audio:** Starts in near-silence. A very low sub-frequency swell of deep water fades in, closed and muffled. **No music.**
- **Voice:** NARRATOR — KJV (0:00.5–0:05): *"In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."*
- **Why it hooks:** it opens on the most recognizable words ever written, with the voice starting in the first half-second over an image that is visibly *moving* toward something. Don't hold on static black.

**KEYFRAME PROMPT**
```
Vertical 9:16. Near-total darkness. An empty, featureless black void fills the upper
four-fifths of the frame. At the very bottom edge, the barely perceptible surface of a
vast, still primordial ocean, visible only as a faint, low-contrast blue-black sheen on the
crests of long, heavy swells. No horizon line, no land, no stars, no moon, no light source
anywhere. Extremely low-key exposure, deep blue-black values. Style: prestige hand-drawn TV
anime background art, painterly brush texture within the darkness, subtle film grain.
Mood: vast, silent, primordial.
```
**MOTION PROMPT**
```
Slow, steady camera tilt downward from the empty black void to the ocean surface over
6 seconds. Swells move almost imperceptibly: long-period, heavy, slow. Nothing else moves.
Keep still: exposure and darkness level constant throughout.
```
**AVOID:** stars, nebula, space imagery, particles, glowing light, lens flare, visible horizon, camera shake.

- **Audio:** Deep water: slow rolling swells, the hollow slap of heavy water, low wind. All of it slightly muffled, as if the world has no air yet. **No music.**

### E01-S03 · 0:14–0:22 · "THE SPIRIT OF GOD MOVED"
- **Visual:** The camera travels low over the water with an invisible moving front. **Ahead** of it, the surface is rough and dark. **Where it has passed**, the water lies in a broad path of glassy, flattened calm, and a fine veil of spray lifts and settles in its wake. This is the Spirit's signature (Bible §6): presence shown by effect.
- **Camera:** 24mm, about 1 m above the surface. **Tracking** forward, matching the speed of the front, so the flattened path unrolls beneath the camera.
- **Lighting:** Near-black; the glassy path reflects marginally more than the rough water around it, so the path reads.
- **Tone:** Presence, movement, anticipation.
- **Audio:** A deep, broad rush of moving air over water, rising gently as the front passes. Fine spray hiss. The swell sounds calm behind it. **No music.**
- **Voice:** NARRATOR — KJV (0:14.5–0:20): *"And the Spirit of God moved upon the face of the waters."* Hold 2 seconds after the line.

**KEYFRAME PROMPT**
```
Vertical 9:16. Low shot one meter above a dark primordial ocean at night-like darkness.
A vast invisible wind-front crosses the water ahead of camera: beyond it the surface is
rough and dark; beneath and behind camera the water has been pressed into a broad path of
smooth, glassy calm, with a fine veil of spray lifting and falling along its edges. No
visible figure or form. The glassy path catches marginally more of the faint ambient light
than the rough water around it. 24mm lens, water filling the lower half. Near-black blue
values. Style: prestige hand-drawn TV anime background art, painterly, physically realistic
water behavior, subtle film grain.
```
**MOTION PROMPT**
```
Camera tracks forward low over the water at steady speed, following the moving wind-front.
The front flattens the rough surface as it advances, leaving glassy calm behind it; fine
spray lifts and falls with realistic weight. 8 seconds.
Keep still: no figure, no light change.
```
**AVOID:** dove, ghostly figure, face in the water, glowing trail, magic particles, light beams, sparkles.

### E01-S04a · 0:22–0:27 · SILENCE → "LET THERE BE LIGHT"
- **Visual:** A locked-off wide at water level in total darkness, as in S02. For 2.5 seconds, nothing but slow swells. On GOD's words, **the entire water surface shivers**: a fine, even tremor across all of it at once, with micro-ripples everywhere. It is not a shockwave and not an explosion. The world responds to the voice.
- **Camera:** 24mm, locked-off.
- **Audio:** **Total silence at 0:22.** All sound drops out, even the water, for about 2.5 seconds. On "Let there be light," a faint, fine hiss of the rippling surface, very quiet.
- **Voice:**
  - NARRATOR — KJV (0:24.5–0:25.3): *"And God said,"*
  - GOD — KJV (0:25.5–0:26.8): *"Let there be light:"*
- **Delivery note:** GOD speaks at conversational volume, calm and unhurried. The awe comes from the silence around the words, not from volume.

**KEYFRAME PROMPT**
```
Vertical 9:16. Locked-off low wide shot at water level of a dark, endless primordial
ocean in near-total darkness, identical framing to shot E01-S02. Heavy slow swells, faint
blue-black sheen, no horizon. 24mm lens. Extremely low-key. Style: prestige hand-drawn TV
anime background art, painterly, subtle film grain.
```
**MOTION PROMPT**
```
Locked-off camera. For the first 2.5 seconds, only slow heavy swells move. Then the
entire water surface shivers at once with a fine, even tremor: tiny ripples across the
whole surface simultaneously, like a vibration passing through the water, not a wave or
shockwave. The tremor continues to the end. 5 seconds.
Keep still: camera, darkness level. No light yet.
```
**AVOID:** explosion, shockwave ring, flash, light, glow, particles.

### E01-S04b · 0:27–0:30 · "AND THERE WAS LIGHT"
- **Visual:** Same frame. **Light arrives from everywhere at once, not from any point.** Black thins to deep blue, then grey, then brilliant white. The exposure blooms to near-white for about one second, then settles to reveal the Deep in soft, even, sourceless light. **No sun, no beam, no rays, no flare.** The tremor on the water stills as the light settles.
- **Generation:** Use first-frame/last-frame control. The **first frame** is the last frame of S04a; the **last frame** is the S05 keyframe look (lit water under luminous mist, same framing as S04a).
- **Camera:** Locked-off.
- **Audio — the key sound design idea:** Before the light, the world sounded closed, muffled and low-passed. **When the light arrives, the sound opens to its full spectrum**: air, detailed spray, the shape of the swells. No whoosh, no boom. In the same moment, **M-01 (Creation) enters:** a low string chord blooming up from nothing.
- **Voice:** NARRATOR — KJV (0:28–0:29.5): *"and there was light."* It lands as the light settles, not during the flash.

**KEYFRAME PROMPT (last frame)**
```
Vertical 9:16. The same low wide framing at water level as shot E01-S04a, now fully lit
by soft, even, sourceless white light that comes from no single direction. Endless slate
grey-green ocean with pale foam lines along swell crests. Above the water, a luminous white
mist with no clear boundary between air and water: no clouds, no blue sky, no sun, no
visible light source. 24mm lens. Style: prestige hand-drawn TV anime background art,
painterly, restrained natural color, subtle film grain.
```
**MOTION PROMPT**
```
Locked-off camera. Starting from darkness, light rises evenly across the entire frame at
once, from no point source: black thins to deep blue, then grey, then bright white,
briefly near-overexposed for about one second, then settling into soft even light revealing
the ocean in color. The tremor on the water surface calms as the light settles. 3 seconds.
Keep still: camera. No sun, no rays.
```
**AVOID:** sun, sun disk, sunrise, light beams, god rays, lens flare, starburst, light orb, explosion, particles.

- **Camera:** 50mm at the start, close on the surface; tilts up to reveal the scale.
- **Lighting:** Sourceless, even. Brightest in the mist, a little deeper on the water.
- **Tone:** Awe; stillness; goodness.
- **Audio:** Full-spectrum water and air, gentle and calm. **M-01:** a wordless solo voice states the Creation motif for the first time (a slow rising figure in open fifths) over the low strings.
- **Voice:** NARRATOR — KJV (0:31–0:35): *"And God saw the light, that it was good:"*

## CONTINUITY NOTES (what later episodes must honor)
1. **E-01 look is now locked:** slate grey-green water, pale foam lines, and a luminous mist with no boundary between sea and air. Episode 2 opens on this exact look.
2. **No sun, moon, stars, clouds or blue sky** until the firmament (Ep 2) for sky, and Day 4 for the lights.
3. **The Spirit's signature** (a moving front leaving a glassy, flattened path) is established. Reuse it at Genesis 8:1.
4. **The light is sourceless and even.** This is the visual definition of divine light for the series.
5. **The sound rule** (closed and muffled in darkness, full spectrum in light) can recur at major divine moments.
6. **Voices locked:** the NARRATOR and GOD voices chosen for this episode are permanent for the series.
7. **M-01 (Creation)** is established. Its opening interval returns in the Covenant motif (M-04).
8. **Screen orientation:** in S06–S08, light is on the left and darkness on the right. Keep that orientation if Episode 2 revisits the boundary.
