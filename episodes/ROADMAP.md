# GENESIS — MASTER ROADMAP

The master numbering (1–148) is fixed. When a roadmap episode cannot fit in **61–90 seconds** without cutting divine speech, it splits into lettered parts (`02A`, `02B`, …). See Bible §1.2 and §13.

**Arc I expanded from 10 to 18 episodes.** Genesis 1:1–3:24 is 2,124 KJV words; at the Bible's narration pace (about 120 wpm plus silence), that needs 18 episodes of 61–90 seconds to keep every word of divine speech and dialogue. Later arcs will be re-cut the same way when their batch is planned, so expect the final count to exceed 148.
