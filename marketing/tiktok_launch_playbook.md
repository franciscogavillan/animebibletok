# @AnimeBibleTok — TikTok Launch Playbook

*Researched September 2026. Platform rules change often, so re-check the Creator Rewards terms in TikTok's Creator Academy before relying on any payout assumption.*

---

## 0. Read this first: monetization risk with AI visuals + AI voice

Several 2026 sources, and creator reports, say TikTok's Creator Rewards Program (CRP) **excludes fully AI-generated videos** ("minimal original input"). Some creators report AI-visual videos being flagged as *unoriginal*: no payout, reduced reach. AI used as a *tool* (grading, captions, some B-roll) stays eligible. AI as the *primary creator* (AI visuals plus AI voiceover) is the risky case. **Failing to label AI content** carries escalating penalties, up to removal from the program.

**What protects this channel:**
1. **Always turn on TikTok's "AI-generated content" label.** It is required, and hiding it is what gets accounts removed from the program.
2. **Replace the AI narrator with a human voice.** This is the biggest lever. Record the KJV narration yourself, or hire a voice actor, and keep the performance notes in Bible §9. A human performance is clear original input, and it usually lifts retention as well. (Keep the GOD voice human too, or at least human-directed.)
3. **Make the authorship visible:** the original script adaptation, shot design, sound design, edit and burned-in captions are all yours. A pinned comment or a short behind-the-scenes video ("how we storyboard Genesis") helps viewers and reviewers see the human work.
4. **Don't depend on CRP alone.** It needs 10k followers and 100k views in 30 days before it pays anything. Plan for Subscriptions/LIVE gifts, YouTube Shorts plus long-form compilations (YouTube monetizes long-form differently), and a later paid "full Genesis" compilation.

**Profile photo:** the AnimeBibleTok logo badge (already the watermark, so recognition is consistent).

**Pinned videos (3):** Episode 1 · a 20–30 s series trailer (make it once Arc I has footage) · the newest episode.

**Playlist:** create a playlist named **"GENESIS — The Anime (in order)"** and add every episode in story order. Playlists show on the profile and on each video, so a viewer who finishes one episode is offered the next. This is the most important structural tool for a serialized show.

## 2. Hashtags

TikTok hashtags are now mostly a **topic signal** for the recommender and for TikTok Search, not a discovery feed. Use **4–6 per post**:

| Slot | Use | Tags |
|---|---|---|
| Series (always) | Brand and binge path | `#animebibletok` |
| Core (always, pick 3) | What the video is | `#bible` `#anime` `#genesis` `#kjv` |
| Community (rotate 1) | Faith audience | `#christiantiktok` `#bibletok` `#biblestory` `#scripture` `#faith` |
| Episode-specific (1–2) | Search terms for this story | Ep 1–5: `#creation` `#inthebeginning` · Eden: `#adamandeve` `#gardenofeden` · Fall: `#theserpent` `#originalsin` · Arc II: `#cainandabel` `#noahsark` · Joseph: `#josephandhisbrothers` |
| Anime crossover (optional 1) | Reach anime viewers | `#animeart` `#animetiktok` `#christiananime` |

**Default for Episode 1:** `#genesis #bible #anime #kjv #creation #animebibletok`

**Skip** `#fyp`, `#foryou`, `#viral` and long walls of tags. They carry no topic signal and make the post look spammy.

## 3. What the algorithm rewards (2026) and how this series uses it

| Signal (by weight) | Target | How we hit it |
|---|---|---|
| **Completion rate and watch time** (≈40–50% of ranking; "working" threshold now about 70% completion) | 70%+ | Episodes are 61–90 s with a scripted hook, a climax and an end card. Captions are burned in, so viewers watching muted still follow. |
| **First 3 seconds** | Stop the scroll | Every episode opens on its strongest KJV line with the voice starting in the first 0.5 s. Use the **cover image** for the most striking frame (for Ep 1: the golden light breaking at 0:29, not the black opening). |
| **Rewatches** | Loops | The end card fades back toward the opening mood, so episodes loop cleanly. Captions reward a second watch because the KJV language is dense and beautiful. |
| **Shares and saves** | "Save this" content | Scripture content is highly saveable. The post caption states the verse range so people save it as a reference. |
| **Comments** | Real conversation | Ask one genuine question per post (see §5). Reply to the best comments **with video**, e.g. a 15 s clip answering "why no apple?" from the Bible's fidelity notes. |
| **Niche consistency** | One clear niche | Every post is the series. No off-topic posts on this account. |

## 4. Posting cadence and launch plan

**Bank before you launch.** Have **7–10 episodes finished before posting Episode 1**, so you can post daily for the first 10 days while the account is being classified. A new account that posts daily in one niche is learned fastest.

| Phase | Cadence | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Launch (days 1–10) | 1 episode/day, same hour | Ep 01 → 05 covers Creation, a complete mini-arc |
| Growth | 4–5 per week | Keep the same posting hour; Sunday is strong for faith content |
| Always | Reply-with-video 2–3× per week | Uses comments to create extra, cheap, high-intent posts |

**Timing:** start with 7–9 pm in your main audience's time zone, and test one lunchtime slot (12–1 pm). Keep whichever wins by 3-second hold rate, not by views.

**Cross-post the same master:** YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels (strip the TikTok watermark; the in-video @AnimeBibleTok badge stays). Later, a **YouTube long-form "Genesis 1–3: Creation & Eden"** compilation of Arc I (about 23 min).

## 6. TikTok Search (SEO)

TikTok is a search engine for "Bible story" queries. Put the searchable words in three places:
1. **The first line of the caption** (the verse text or reference).
2. **Burned-in captions**: already done, since TikTok reads on-screen text.
3. **The cover text**: `EP 1 · IN THE BEGINNING`.

Keywords to rotate naturally into captions: *Genesis 1 KJV, creation story, Bible anime, Bible story animated, Adam and Eve anime, Garden of Eden, Noah's ark anime, KJV Bible.*

---

## 7. Don'ts (these hurt reach or eligibility)

- Don't hide or skip the AI-content label.
- Don't buy followers or views, or join engagement pods.
- Don't delete and repost underperforming videos repeatedly.
- Don't post watermarked re-uploads from other platforms.
- Don't use trending sounds *over* the narration. The KJV voice is the product. (Trending sounds are fine for separate behind-the-scenes posts.)
- Don't add false cliffhangers or clickbait text that misstates Scripture. It breaks the series' trust promise ("every word KJV").
